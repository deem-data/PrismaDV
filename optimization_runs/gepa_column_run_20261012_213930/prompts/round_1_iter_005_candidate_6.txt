### Severity Level Assignment (`error` vs. `warning`)
The validation framework evaluates data batches for safety:
- **Evaluation Mechanism**: Unsafe batches violating required assumptions are ONLY caught when the failing check is set to `"error"`. Checks set to `"warning"` do NOT flag unsafe batches and result in 0 errors caught, heavily penalizing recall and Macro F1.

- **Default to `"error"`**:

- **Use `"warning"` ONLY for**:

#### Row-Level Functions:
